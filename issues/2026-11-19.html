Microsoft · MAI Image 2.7
NewImageEditing

Microsoft's MAI Image 2.7 aims squarely at image editing

Announced at Ignite on 17 Nov, MAI Image 2.7 and a faster Flash variant are in public preview on Microsoft Foundry and through partners including fal, OpenRouter and Vercel. Microsoft says the full model ranks first on Artificial Analysis' image-editing leaderboard, ahead of OpenAI's Sunburst, at around a fifth of the cost: roughly $35 per 1,000 images, or $10 for Flash.

The pitch is targeted edits: change an object's colour, material or position without disturbing the rest of the frame, and keep consistency across a chain of edits. Both models can output transparent backgrounds. A new Canvas workspace is built around Flash.

WHY IT MATTERSIterative, local edits with stable surroundings are what product shots and key-art variations need. Native alpha output means icons, logos and packshots can drop into a comp without a roto or keying pass. The rankings are Microsoft's own citation, so test it on your own material.

OpenAI GPT-6.2

GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol

OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra (frontier) and Sol (everyday) on 17 Nov. Astra can rebuild slides from a file or screenshot, matching layout and typography, and builds stronger front-ends for 3D and web. Astra's API price drops 20%.

Runway Praxis-1

World models: Runway Praxis-1

Runway released Praxis-1, a 27B open-weights world action model for robot control, under Apache 2.0. Its benchmark lead is self-reported. Notable as a video-gen company pushing its world-model work beyond pictures.

Qwen4-Max · Hy4 flash

Multimodal models from China

Alibaba made Qwen4-Max generally available internationally, covering text, image and video understanding, and Tencent released Hy4 flash, a cheaper multimodal model with a one-million-token context.
