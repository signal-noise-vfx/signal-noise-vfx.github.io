Signal/Noise

AI news for VFX, motion and promo people: only what changes your work, explained in a few lines.

TL;DR, 30 SECONDS

  • The After Effects AI Assistant has shipped. It automates repetitive tasks by text prompt and writes expressions.
  • Adobe is opening AE to outside AI agents through MCP.
  • Seedance 2.5 now renders in 4K on Runway and Pika.
  • Google video generation got cheaper and longer, and Flow got an agent.

On your desk · tools

01
After Effects
Must tryAfter Effects

The After Effects AI Assistant ships in the November release

The November 2026 release adds an agentic AI Assistant. You control After Effects with natural-language instructions, and it can automate repetitive work or generate JavaScript expressions. It was in beta since August and won CineD's Best of Show for AI innovation at IBC.

Adobe also announced at MAX that it will support MCP (Model Context Protocol), so you can connect third-party AI agents directly to After Effects.

WHY IT MATTERSIt targets exactly your team's pain point: versioning, renaming and expressions for promo packages. With MCP, an agent like Claude could drive AE directly.
02
Adobe Firefly
Adobe MAXFirefly

Adobe repositions Firefly as an "AI creative studio"

At MAX (10–12 Nov, Miami Beach), the focus was AI agents everywhere. Firefly now brings Adobe's tools, third-party models and a Creative Agent together in one place. The Firefly Video editor goes from a story idea to a cut, with captions and generated transitions. A new Firefly Design workspace (beta) puts illustration, images, motion, 3D and print on one canvas, with live-linked PSD and AI files.

WHY IT MATTERSExpect more third-party models (Google and others) inside the apps you already pay for. Fewer separate subscriptions, but more lock-in to Adobe.
03
Seedance 2.5 · 4K
Video genSeedance

Seedance 2.5 arrives in 4K on Runway and Pika

Pika and Runway each announced 4K for ByteDance's Seedance 2.5 on 11 November. The model already supports long, reference-heavy clips: up to 30 seconds, with up to 50 references in a single generation.

WHY IT MATTERS4K output from a top-ranked model moves AI plates and inserts closer to on-air quality. You'll still need to fix temporal flicker in comp.
04
Google Flow
GoogleFlow

Google Flow gets an agent, and Gemini video gets cheaper

Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash is now generally available. It makes 15-second clips and costs 30% less than the previous version, at $0.07 per second for 720p. Flow added a Flow Agent for planning and editing projects, custom workflows built with natural language, and mobile apps starting on Android.

WHY IT MATTERSPrices are falling fast, so cheap rough animatics and mood clips for pitches are realistic now.
05
OpenAI · Images 2.6
OpenAIImage

ChatGPT Images 2.6: twice as fast, and it makes GIFs

OpenAI's new image model is 50% faster and can now create animated GIFs.

WHY IT MATTERSIt's minor for broadcast, but useful for quick social teasers and internal pitch decks.
Bloomway

👀 Worth watching: 3D previs → AI video

Bloomway's new 3D Director Studio lets you build an environment, block characters and set camera moves with text. The animated previs then guides the AI video generation. This is the direction "directable" AI video is going.

⚠️ Heads-up: model retired

BytePlus shut down Seedance 1.5 Pro on 11 November with no automatic migration. If any pipeline or API script uses it, it's broken now.

Big picture · models & research

Anthropic

Claude Fable 5.2 & Mythos 5.2

Anthropic, 9 Nov. They share one base model: Fable is for all users and Mythos for verified organizations. Up to 30% faster than the previous generation.

Gemini 4

Gemini 4 Carbon & Hydrogen

Google released Gemini 4 Carbon to general availability on 10 Nov, followed by Gemini 4 Hydrogen on 12 Nov.

JEPA · world models

JEPA corner: H-JEPA

Introduced in October, H-JEPA learns at several levels of abstraction at once, so one world model can handle both immediate control and long-term planning. LeCun's AMI Labs, backed by a $1.03B seed round, is betting on this line of work.
