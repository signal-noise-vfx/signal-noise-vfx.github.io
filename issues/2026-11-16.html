MiniMax opens up H3-Image: native 4K stills, but read the licence

MiniMax published the weights of H3-Image (11 Nov), the stills sibling of its H3 video model. It is a 33-billion-parameter diffusion model that outputs around 16 MP natively, including wide 21:9 frames, in roughly 10–16 seconds through the API. Setup notes cover ComfyUI and diffusers.

The catch is the licence: larger companies and anyone serving third parties need written permission, and the licence explicitly excludes the EU, UK, US and South Korea.

WHY IT MATTERSNative 4K and 21:9 straight out of the model means fewer upscale passes for backplates and key art. But as an EU broadcaster you cannot use the open weights commercially without a deal, so treat it as a test bench, not a production tool.

Pika releases H3 Flex as an open-weights video model

Pika released H3 Flex with open weights. On DesignArena it currently tops the board for open-weights text-to-video models.

WHY IT MATTERSOpen video models can run on your own GPUs, so footage and briefs never leave the building. That matters for embargoed promo material. Check the licence terms before any on-air use.

OpenAI · Images 2.6 API
OpenAIImage

Images 2.6 reaches the API as two models: Flare and Sunburst

Following last issue’s ChatGPT update, the API now offers two image models: Flare for speed and Sunburst for quality. ChatGPT also gained SVG export alongside GIF output.

WHY IT MATTERSSVG export is the useful part for you: rough logo marks and icons that go straight into Illustrator or AE as shape layers, instead of raster you have to trace.

⚠️ Heads-up: Kling 4.0 rollout

Kling 4.0 (30-second clips, 10-keyframe control, 4K 10-bit HDR in the full version) rolled out in stages, with Flash at 720p first. If you tested it early, you probably saw the Flash tier, not the final quality.

Meta Muse Ember

Meta released Muse Ember on 12 Nov, the smallest model in its Muse family, with closed weights.

JEPA · world models

JEPA corner: JEPA-Anything

An academic team (NUS, Shanghai Jiao Tong, Edinburgh and others) trained one JEPA-style world model across seven domains, from vision and control to weather and molecules. It beat JEPA baselines on all ten matched tasks and splits dynamics into readable factors.

Open weights

Trend: open models catch up

In one fortnight, MiniMax (image) and Pika (video) both released competitive open-weights models. Closed services still lead, but the gap for self-hosted generation is shrinking, while licences are getting tighter.
